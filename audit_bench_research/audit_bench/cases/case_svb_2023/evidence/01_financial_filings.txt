=== COMPANY: Silicon Valley Bank (SVB) — subsidiary of SVB Financial Group (Nasdaq: SIVB) ===
=== FISCAL YEAR: 2022 (year ended Dec 31, 2022) ===
=== AUDITOR: KPMG LLP ===
=== AUDITOR TENURE: Since the early 1990s (20+ years; SVB was a long-tenured KPMG client) ===

=== EXCERPT: SVB Financial Group 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheet (Dec 31, 2022) ===
Total assets: $212 billion (up from $71B in 2020 — 3x growth in 2 years)
Securities (held-to-maturity, HTM): $91.3 billion (predominantly long-dated US Treasuries and mortgage-backed securities)
Securities (available-for-sale, AFS): $26.1 billion
Total loans: $73.6 billion
Total deposits: $173.2 billion (concentrated in tech/VC-backed startups)

=== EXCERPT: SVB 10-K, Footnote on HTM Portfolio (Dec 31, 2022) ===
"A portion of the securities portfolio is classified as held-to-maturity. The unrealized loss position on the HTM portfolio at Dec 31, 2022 was approximately $15.1 billion (net of tax). The fair value of the HTM portfolio at Dec 31, 2022 was approximately $76.2 billion vs. amortized cost of $91.3 billion."

=== EXCERPT: SVB 10-K, Note on Deposits (Dec 31, 2022) ===
"Of total deposits, approximately 87% were uninsured (i.e., exceeded the FDIC $250k limit) at Dec 31, 2022."
"Approximately $74 billion of deposits were held by tech and life-science/healthcare venture-backed clients; deposit concentrations in the innovation sector exceeded 50% of total deposits."

=== EXCERPT: SVB 10-K, Liquidity Risk Management ===
"SVB relies on a concentrated deposit base. A significant withdrawal of deposits could require the Company to sell securities at a loss."
"Net interest income is sensitive to changes in interest rates. A 200 basis point increase in rates was projected to reduce NII by approximately 8% over the next 12 months."

=== EXCERPT: SVB 10-K, Federal Reserve Actions in 2022 ===
"The Federal Reserve raised the federal funds rate by 425 basis points during the year ended December 31, 2022, with subsequent increases in 2023."

=== EXCERPT: KPMG Audit Opinion (issued Feb 24, 2023) ===
KPMG issued an unqualified (clean) audit opinion for FY2022. No going-concern paragraph. No emphasis-of-matter paragraph regarding unrealized losses or deposit concentration.

=== EXCERPT: SVB 10-K, Going Concern Disclosure ===
No going-concern language. Management asserted ability to meet obligations for the next 12 months.

=== EXCERPT: SVB 10-K, Asset-Liability Duration ===
"HTM portfolio duration was approximately 6.2 years at Dec 31, 2022."
"Deposit duration was near zero (demand deposits)."

=== EXCERPT: SVB CEO Stock Sales ===
"CEO Greg Becker sold $3.6 million of SVB Financial Group stock on February 27, 2023, under a pre-arranged Rule 10b5-1 trading plan adopted on January 26, 2023."

=== KEY DATES ===
Feb 24, 2023 — KPMG issues clean opinion for FY2022.
Mar 8, 2023 — SVB announces $1.75B capital raise + $21B securities portfolio sale at a $1.8B loss.
Mar 9, 2023 — Bank run; $42B of deposits withdrawn in one day.
Mar 10, 2023 — SVB closed by California DFI; FDIC appointed receiver.
