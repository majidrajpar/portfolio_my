=== COMPANY: Enron Corporation (NYSE: ENE) ===
=== FISCAL YEAR: 2000 (year ended Dec 31, 2000) ===
=== AUDITOR: Arthur Andersen LLP ===
=== AUDITOR TENURE: Since 1985 (16 years) ===

=== EXCERPT: Enron 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheet (Dec 31, 2000) ===
Total assets: $65.5 billion
Total liabilities: $54.0 billion
Total equity: $11.5 billion
Revenue (2000): $100.8 billion (vs. $40.1B in 1999, $13.3B in 1998 — tripling in two years)
Net income (2000): $979 million

=== EXCERPT: Enron 10-K, Selected Notes ===
Note 1 — "Enron operates a market-based risk management business (EnronOnline) and a wholesale energy services business."
Note 2 — Equity earnings from unconsolidated special-purpose entities (SPEs): $425 million in 2000.
Note 14 — Related-party transactions: "Enron engages in transactions with partnerships and other entities controlled by the Chief Financial Officer of Enron. These transactions include the sale of financial assets, equity investments, and hedging arrangements."
Note 14 — Aggregate value of transactions with CFO-controlled entities: not fully quantified; disclosed as "material."

=== EXCERPT: Enron 10-K, Revenue Recognition ===
"Enron recognizes revenue from energy contracts and risk management activities at the time the underlying commodity is delivered or the contract is settled. Revenues from price-risk management contracts are reported gross (i.e., at the notional value of the contract)."

=== EXCERPT: Enron Proxy Statement (filed April 2001) ===
Total compensation paid to CFO Andrew Fastow in 2000: not fully disclosed in standard comp tables.
Footnote references compensation through "affiliated partnerships" without dollar value.

=== EXCERPT: Arthur Andersen Engagement Letter (obtained via public filings) ===
"Audit fees: $25 million. Non-audit consulting fees: $27 million (tax, internal audit outsourcing, systems advisory)."

=== EXCERPT: Enron 10-K, Cash Flow Statement (Dec 31, 2000) ===
Operating cash flow: $4.8 billion
Investing cash flow: $(5.2) billion
Net cash from financing: $5.1 billion (heavily reliant on debt issuance and asset sales to SPEs)
"Proceeds from sales of assets to unconsolidated affiliates: $1.2 billion"

=== EXCERPT: Wall Street Journal, October 16-22, 2001 (public reporting emerging) ===
"Enron reports a $618 million third-quarter charge and a $1.2 billion write-down in equity related to transactions with partnerships run by CFO Fastow. SEC announces inquiry. Enron stock falls from $33 to $15."

=== EXCERPT: Enron 10-K, Going Concern Statement ===
No going-concern language. Arthur Andersen issued an unqualified ('clean') audit opinion on Feb 2, 2001 — eight months before the third-quarter charge.

=== EXCERPT: Analyst Reports, Q1-Q3 2001 ===
Of 17 sell-side analysts covering Enron, 15 maintained 'Buy' or 'Strong Buy' ratings through Q3 2001.
One analyst (Chung Wu at UBS PaineWebber) downgraded to 'Neutral' in August 2001; he was reassigned off the Enron account the next week.

=== KEY DATES ===
Feb 2, 2001 — Andersen issues clean audit opinion for FY2000.
Oct 16, 2001 — Enron announces $618M charge + $1.2B equity write-down from CFO partnerships.
Oct 31, 2001 — SEC formal investigation.
Dec 2, 2001 — Enron files Chapter 11.
