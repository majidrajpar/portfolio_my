=== COMPANY: Wirecard AG (Deutsche Boerse: WDIG) ===
=== FISCAL YEAR: 2019 (year ended Dec 31, 2019) ===
=== AUDITOR: Ernst & Young GmbH, Munich ===
=== AUDITOR TENURE: Since 2009 (10+ years) ===

=== EXCERPT: Wirecard 2019 Annual Report, Consolidated Balance Sheet (Dec 31, 2019) ===
Total assets: EUR 5.7 billion
Cash and cash equivalents: EUR 1.9 billion (reported as held in "trust accounts" with third-party acquiring banks)
Total liabilities: EUR 2.6 billion
Total equity: EUR 3.1 billion
Revenue (2019): EUR 1.7 billion (up from EUR 532M in 2015 — 3.2x growth in 4 years)
Net income (2019): EUR 165 million

=== EXCERPT: Wirecard 2019 Annual Report, Note on Cash Balances ===
"Cash and cash equivalents include EUR 1.9 billion held in trust accounts by Wirecard's acquiring banking partners in the Asia-Pacific region. These funds represent merchant settlement balances and customer funds processed through third-party acquirers."

=== EXCERPT: KPMG Special Audit (commissioned by Wirecard in 2019, report April 2020) ===
KPMG was commissioned by the Wirecard board to perform a 'special audit' (Pruefung) of 2015-2018 accounts following Financial Times reporting.
KPMG report (publicly available April 2020): "For the years 2016-2018, KPMG was unable to obtain sufficient and appropriate audit evidence for the existence, completeness, and valuation of approximately EUR 1 billion of consolidated revenue and cash balances processed via third-party acquirers. Trust accounts could not be confirmed by independent third parties."

=== EXCERPT: Financial Times (Dan McCrum, October 2015 - April 2019, public reporting series) ===
2015: FT questions whether Wirecard's Asia-Pacific revenue is supported by real business.
2018: FT reports that Wirecard's Singapore office appears to have inflated revenue and falsified documents.
2019: FT asks the board why third-party acquirer balances cannot be independently confirmed.

=== EXCERPT: EY Audit Opinion (issued March 2018 for FY2017, similar for FY2018/FY2019) ===
EY issued unqualified (clean) audit opinions for FY2017, FY2018, and FY2019.
The 2019 audit opinion for EY Munich was signed in March 2020.

=== EXCERPT: BaFin (German regulator) and APAS actions ===
In 2019, BaFin opened two investigations against Financial Times journalists for market manipulation (later dropped).
In 2019, APAS (German audit oversight body) opened a preliminary investigation into EY's Wirecard audits; no findings published until after collapse.

=== EXCERPT: Wirecard 2019 Annual Report, Related-Party Disclosures ===
No related-party transactions disclosed. No disclosure of fees paid to a Panamanian intermediation company.
Wirecard's business model: ~50% of 2019 revenue routed through third-party acquirers (TPAs) in the Philippines, Singapore, and India.

=== EXCERPT: Corporate Governance ===
CEO Markus Braun (in post since 2002) and COO Jan Marsalek were the principal decision-makers. Both resigned in June 2020.
No audit committee charter disclosed TPA due-diligence procedures.

=== KEY DATES ===
March 2018 — EY issues clean opinion for FY2017.
March 2019 — EY issues clean opinion for FY2018.
March 2020 — EY issues clean opinion for FY2019 (AUDIT OPINION BEING EVALUATED).
April 2020 — KPMG special audit report concludes insufficient evidence for TPA balances.
June 18, 2020 — EY informs Wirecard board that EUR 1.9B of trust cash 'probably does not exist'.
June 22, 2020 — Wirecard admits EUR 1.9B does not exist. CEO arrested.
