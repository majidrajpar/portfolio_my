=== COMPANY: Tesco PLC (LSE: TSCO) ===
=== FIRST HALF: 26 weeks ended Aug 23, 2014 ===
=== AUDITOR: PwC (Tesco UK auditor since the 1980s, 30+ years) ===
=== SCOPE OF ERROR: Commercial income (supplier rebates) overstatement ===

=== EXCERPT: Tesco interim results, Sept 1, 2014 (pre-warning) ===
Trading profit (UK & ROI): reported as £1.1 billion for H1 2014.
Commercial income line: £202 million for H1 2014.
Commercial income description: "Income earned from suppliers in respect of promotional contributions, rebates, and supplier investment in trade."

=== EXCERPT: Tesco Press Release, Sept 22, 2014 ===
"Tesco has identified an overstatement of approximately £263 million in commercial income in the first half of 2014/15. The overstatement relates principally to the accrual of commercial income. The half-year results previously announced on 1 September 2014 overstated profits by approximately 23%."

=== EXCERPT: Deloitte forensic investigation (commissioned by Tesco, Oct 2014) ===
Deloitte found: "The overstatement arose from the recognition of commercial income on a 'gross' basis in periods where the offsetting promotional costs had not been incurred. Rebate income was recognised in advance of the underlying customer behaviour that would trigger the rebate."
Three senior executives suspended pending investigation.
Eight auditors (PwC) had signed off the H1 2014 commercial income without independent supplier confirmation.

=== EXCERPT: Tesco 2013/14 Annual Report, Supplier Agreements ===
"Commercial income is recognised when the relevant sales to customers occur. Recognition is based on agreements with suppliers and is calculated on a consistent basis to the prior year."
Note: actual practice diverged — Tesco UK recognised rebates based on *forecast* customer behaviour, not actuals.

=== EXCERPT: PwC Audit Workpapers (later examined by FRC) ===
PwC obtained supplier rebates confirmations for FY2013/14 from a sample of 19 suppliers out of >2,000.
Of the 19 confirmations requested, 12 were obtained; 7 were not returned and follow-up was limited.
Sample covered approximately 8% of commercial income by value.

=== EXCERPT: FRC Tribunal Findings (2017-2019) ===
The Financial Reporting Council brought a case against PwC and senior engagement partner Stephen Duenwald over the FY2013/14 audit.
Findings: PwC failed to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence for commercial income. Reliance on management representations without independent confirmation. No consideration of fraud risk specific to commercial income (a known high-fraud-risk area for retailers).

=== EXCERPT: Tesco Financial Calendar ===
Sep 1, 2014 — Tesco announces H1 results (showing inflated £202M commercial income).
Sep 22, 2014 — Tesco issues profit warning, suspends 4 senior UK executives, admits £263M overstatement.
Oct 1, 2014 — FRC opens investigation into PwC's FY2013/14 audit.

=== EXCERPT: PwC Audit Approach to Commercial Income (per FRC ruling) ===
PwC had audited Tesco since the 1980s. The commercial income accrual was tested through:
- Comparisons to prior-year trends.
- Management representations.
- Limited supplier confirmations (sample size not adjusted for growth in commercial income).
No substantive analytical procedures specific to the rebate accrual methodology change in 2013/14.
